Turn lab notebooks and notes into monthly progress reports for your supervisor, and structure progress, plans and risks for annual, transfer or confirmation reviews.

## Problem

Monthly reports to your supervisor and annual or transfer reviews arrive at busy times; reconstructing progress from lab notebooks and scattered notes and fitting it to the required headings takes days.

## Result

A draft under your form's headings with gaps marked [TO ADD], a risk register and evidence of skills development – a starting point you complete with verified facts.

## Steps

1. Paste the form's headings and assessment criteria
2. Add bullet notes or lab-notebook entries and earlier objectives
3. Ask for a risk register with likelihood and impact
4. Map training to the Researcher Development Framework
5. Check every achievement and date against your records

## Concepts

### Vitae Researcher Development Framework (RDF)

A UK framework of researcher competencies in four domains: knowledge and intellectual abilities; personal effectiveness; research governance and organisation; engagement, influence and impact. Use it to evidence training and skills development in reviews.

### Risk register and likelihood–impact matrix

Each risk is listed with likelihood and impact (for example scored 1–5), their product as priority, a mitigation and an owner, following the logic of ISO 31000 risk management. Review it at each milestone.

### SMART objectives

Doran's (1981) criteria: specific, measurable, assignable or achievable, realistic and time-related. Next year's objectives written this way can be assessed at the following review.

## Own material

### Bring

Bullet-point notes or lab-notebook entries on this period's progress, outputs and plans, and the section headings of your report or review form; leave out health or other personal circumstances.

### Steps

1. Paste the section headings of your monthly report or review form.
2. Paste your notes and ask for a draft under those headings.
3. Ask for a risk table with likelihood, impact and mitigation.
4. Check every achievement and date against your notes and records.

### Prompt

```text
I am a postgraduate researcher in [discipline] preparing my [monthly progress report for my supervisor / annual / transfer / confirmation review]. The report needs these sections: [paste headings]. My notes are: [paste notes]. Draft the report under these headings in a factual, concise style of about [word count] words. Include progress against the objectives of the last period, outputs, training, a plan for the next period (month or year), and a table of risks with likelihood, impact and mitigation. Use only what is in my notes; mark missing information with [TO ADD] and do not invent achievements, dates or results.
```

### Check

Every achievement, output and date in the draft matches your notes, and each risk is one you recognise as real.

## Technology

Paste headings and notes into your AI assistant, or upload last year's report as a Word file for continuity. Leave out health and personal circumstances; gaps appear as [TO ADD], not invented content.

## Levels

### Chat

Run the starter prompt with your headings and notes pasted. In the same chat, ask the assistant to list every statement in the draft next to the note it came from, so you see what rests on your records and what is [TO ADD].

### Project / Notebook

Keep one project or notebook for the year: add your lab-notebook entries or monthly notes, last period's objectives, the review form and your university's skills framework (e.g. the Researcher Development Framework) as references, adding notes each month. At review time ask for a draft under the form's headings and a risk register drawn from the whole year, not from memory.

### Custom assistant

Instructions: your report types and their headings, factual style and word count, progress against previous objectives, a risk table with likelihood, impact and mitigation, [TO ADD] for gaps, never invent achievements, dates or results. Knowledge: the review form and its assessment criteria. Starter prompts: "Monthly report from these notes" and "Draft my annual review". Reusable every month and at every review.
